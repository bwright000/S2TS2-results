Notes

  • Mean PSNR over the six datasets, ours: 24.27 dB before refinement, 26.14 dB after.
  • Online inputs. Ours computes its own flow (522 ms per frame), segmentation with DINOv2 features (1,552 ms) and masks and tool motion (494 ms) on a second thread while the previous frame is tracked and mapped. Only the 278 ms the loop waits for them is in Tracking. Counted in full, as if nothing overlapped, our FPS would be 0.13 without refinement and 0.09 with it.
  • SGS-SLAM is given the dataset's semantic labels as input, at no time cost. Stereo depth is precomputed for every method and is in no row.
  • Benchmark runs of 9 November, one run per sequence, seed 0.
