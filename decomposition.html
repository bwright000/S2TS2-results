Motion decomposition on vs off

Every sequence run with and without motion decomposition on the new code (f682907), seed 0. Each clip plays the sequence twice side by side: the full model on the left, decomposition off on the right. Each side shows the input, the final render, the pixels used for tracking and the camera path against ground truth; the table under the video gives the final results for both.

The tracking mask is rebuilt from each run's log and saved labels: instruments are excluded in the current and previous frame, and the decomposer's verdict on tissue is logged every 20 frames and held in between. With decomposition off no pixel is excluded. Camera path: final estimate after Sim(3) alignment. Tissue and tool PSNR split the final render by the dataset's instrument labels. L4 runs are quality-only.
